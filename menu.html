The menu · autumn

Twelve courses, one long evening.

It follows the market, so it changes every few weeks. This is what we are cooking now. 1,450 kr a person, one seating at 19:00.

  1. 01

    เมี่ยงคำ Miang kham

    Betel leaf, toasted coconut, lime, dried shrimp. One bite, eaten with your hands.

  2. 02

    ข้าวตังหน้าตั้ง Khao tang na tang

    Crisp rice with a relish of pork, peanut and coconut cream.

  3. 03

    ส้มตำ Som tam

    Green papaya pounded at the counter, to your heat.

  4. 04

    กุ้งเผา River prawn over charcoal

    Nam jim seafood, burnt lime, a slice of grilled bread for the juices.

  5. 05

    ต้มยำน้ำข้น Tom yam nam khon

    Langoustine, galangal, roasted chilli paste, a little evaporated milk.

  6. 06

    หอยเชลล์ Raw scallop

    Danish scallop, nam pla wan, makrut lime, crisp shallot.

  7. 07

    ลาบ Larb of venison

    Toasted rice, mint, sawtooth herb, a cabbage leaf to wrap it.

  8. 08

    ปลาเผา Salt-crust fish

    Whole plaice baked in salt and lemongrass, opened at the counter.

  9. 09

    แกงเผ็ด Red curry of roast duck

    Danish plums, Thai basil, jasmine rice from Surin.

  10. 10

    The loud one. Hand-cut beef, holy basil, a wok at full flame.

  11. 11

    Sea buckthorn, palm sugar, toasted sticky rice.

    Butterfly pea, salted coconut cream.

To drink

  • Juice and tea pairing

    550 kr

    Pressed and fermented juices, cold-brewed tea and one tamarind soda. Made here, the week you come.

  • By the glass

    from 110 kr

    A short list of wine, two beers from Nørrebro and a Thai whisky we will not apologise for.

Good to know

Allergies. Tell us when you book and we cook around most of them. We need a week's notice for shellfish, nuts or gluten.

Vegetarian. There is a vegetarian menu with a week's notice. There is no vegan menu: fish sauce runs through everything we make.

Heat. We cook to Bangkok heat by default. Say so and we cook to Copenhagen heat instead. Nobody will judge you.
